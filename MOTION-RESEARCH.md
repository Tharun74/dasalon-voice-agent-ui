# Motion research

1. Gemini Live emphasizes rhythm, pitch and tone. Let phrase energy move the silhouette and let articulation change the highlights. [Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/gemini-live-audio-updates/)
2. ChatGPT Voice can wait through a hesitation and yield to an interruption. Keep a little visual anticipation during pauses and give barge-in a fast recoil. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-live/)
3. Siri’s light traces the edge of the screen. Start listening with a restrained edge highlight before contracting the form. [Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/10/apple-intelligence-is-available-today-on-iphone-ipad-and-mac/)
4. Hume EVI can require sustained speech before yielding. Add hysteresis so a short burst of noise cannot flip the visual state. [Hume EVI](https://dev.hume.ai/voice/docs/speech-to-speech-evi/configuration/interruption)
5. Sesame measures the timing of conversational turns. Test hesitation, overlap and recovery in addition to steady speech. [Sesame TurnBench](https://www.sesame.com/journal/turnbench)
6. LiveKit Aura uses distinct state animations in one field. Give our listening and speaking states opposite directions that read with labels hidden. [LiveKit Aura](https://docs.livekit.io/reference/components/agents-ui/component/agent-audio-visualizer-aura/)
7. ElevenLabs Orb accepts separate input and output levels. Keep independent envelopes for air pulled inward by the user and waves sent outward by the agent. [ElevenLabs Orb](https://ui.elevenlabs.io/docs/components/orb)
8. Aether layers an audio response over a flow field. Give Ink in Water and Murmuration motion of their own, then use syllables to inject energy at specific points. [Codrops Aether walkthrough](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/08/06/building-aether-1-sound-without-boundaries/)
9. Shadertoy’s audio input separates waveform and spectrum. Drive material details with bands and use RMS and peak to control the body and onset accents. [Inigo Quilez’s Shadertoy audio input example](https://sources.debian.org/src/kodi-visualization-shadertoy/21.0.2%2Bds-1/visualization.shadertoy/resources/shaders/input.frag.glsl)
10. Active Theory ties its navigation pill to scroll velocity. Integrate speech-driven speed over time so stronger syllables accelerate a motion already underway. [Active Theory studio interview](https://www.commarts.com/webpicks/active-theory-2)
11. Lusion combines motion and depth in its interactive 3D work. Give Mercury and Jewel one readable silhouette and let reflections trail behind the body’s movement. [Lusion](https://lusion.co/)
12. The Lusion portfolio on Awwwards puts dramatic moments between calmer stretches. Give every preset a distinct idle rhythm and save large overshoot for the most emphatic events. [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/lusion-v3)

# Research Findings

1. Borrowed ElevenLabs Orb’s seeded motion, palette controls and independent input/output getters. Lumen will use deterministic material variation and keep each speaker on its own signal path. [ElevenLabs Orb](https://ui.elevenlabs.io/docs/components/orb), [official repository](https://github.com/elevenlabs/ui)
2. Borrowed LiveKit’s state-specific behavior across Bar, Grid, Radial, Wave and shader-driven Aura. Each salon piece gets a distinct outline and a different response to the same state contract. [LiveKit Agents UI](https://livekit.com/products/agents-ui), [visualizer docs](https://docs.livekit.io/frontends/agents-ui/audio-visualizer/prebuilt/)
3. Borrowed Orb.js’s thin render loop and blended state choreography. Retarget profile weights from the current blend so barge-in stays continuous under rapid changes. [Orb.js](https://github.com/earlbalai/orb.js)
4. Borrowed AiSphere’s simplex displacement, matcap-style lighting and grazing-angle Fresnel. Lumen’s original material treatment pairs a cosmetic-serum core with a thin-film glass shell. [AiSphere](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@sdkrouter/aisphere)
5. Three.js ShaderMaterial supplies view-space transforms and custom uniforms. Keep coherent animation in shader time and upload audio values once per frame. [Three.js ShaderMaterial](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/ShaderMaterial.html)
6. Use GLSL simplex for coherent surface movement and curl noise for swirling flow in Silk and Stardust. Sample a coarse field on lower quality tiers. [WebGL Noise](https://github.com/ashima/webgl-noise), [Bridson et al.](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~rbridson/docs/bridson-siggraph2007-curlnoise.pdf)
7. Borrowed thin-film interference’s angle-dependent color and React Bits’ continuous hue motion. Tie color to surface angle and local thickness on the glass. [Khronos iridescence specification](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF/blob/main/extensions/2.0/Khronos/KHR_materials_iridescence/README.md), [React Bits Iridescence](https://reactbits.dev/backgrounds/iridescence)
8. Give bloom an emissive threshold and limited spread. Preserve dark cores and reserve the brightest values for polished edges and consonant accents. [React Postprocessing Bloom](https://react-postprocessing.docs.pmnd.rs/effects/bloom)
9. Simulate Stardust position and velocity on the GPU with ping-pong textures and a spring towards seeded sphere targets. Use point sprites with depth attenuation for the suspended powder. [Three.js GPUComputationRenderer](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/GPUComputationRenderer.html)
10. Use time-domain RMS and sample-rate-aware FFT bands. A short attack catches syllables, and a longer release lets a sentence finish without flicker. [Web Audio specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/webaudio/)
11. Borrowed Siri’s sound-responsive light and Apple Intelligence’s habit of placing it on the perimeter. Keep the strongest movement away from text, especially in Dock. [Apple Intelligence and Siri](https://www.apple.com/sg/newsroom/2025/03/apple-intelligence-features-are-now-available-in-singapore/), [iOS 18 feature guide](https://www.apple.com/in/ios/ios-18/pdf/iOS_18_All_New_Features_Sept_2024.pdf)
12. Gemini Live’s demo makes interruption part of the conversation. Give every object a brief physical recoil that immediately yields to its listening gesture. [Gemini Live launch demo](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/product-updates/connect-communicate/made-by-google-gemini-ai-live/)
13. ChatGPT’s separate voice mode shows how a single persistent object can hold attention. Keep one focal object per scene and keep captions optional. [OpenAI Voice Mode FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8400625-voice-mode-faq)
14. Borrowed macro beauty photography, generous negative space and editorial type hierarchy from Awwwards beauty and fashion references. The demo reads as a hair atelier from the first frame. [Awwwards editorial and beauty case studies](https://assets.awwwards.com/awards/gallery/2020/11/live-presentation-aude-fred-richard.pdf), [Rino & Pelle design case study](https://www.behance.net/gallery/93549643/Rino-Pelle)
15. Original to this library is a shared inward listening / outward speaking motion grammar applied to seven salon materials. A prescribed grayscale and muted-audio review checks that every pair reads from motion and silhouette alone.

## Design specification

Every object uses continuous state weights, normally 420 ms. Interrupted enters over 320 ms and muted and offline settle over 520 ms. Listening maps to microphone input and speaking maps to agent output. Connecting and thinking run distinct authored loops in silence. Reduce motion by slowing idle clocks and shrinking travel while retaining state legibility.

## Playground art direction

The gallery is the product view. It puts the eight presets together at the same scale so users can compare silhouettes, states and finishes. Maison Serein is the fictional hair and scalp atelier under **In context**. It pairs a full-height evening salon portrait with a plum-black listening stage, fine ruled typography and soft metal colors.

The first pass uses one shared Three.js renderer and CSS or shader glows. Stardust uses vertex-shader advection with a damped spread envelope. The GPU position/velocity simulation in the table is the original polish target.

The portrait was generated with the built-in image generator as [salon-portrait.webp](playground/assets/salon-portrait.webp).

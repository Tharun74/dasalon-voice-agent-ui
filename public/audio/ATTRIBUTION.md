# LibriSpeech recording

`librispeech.flac` is an unmodified human speech sample from the LibriSpeech ASR corpus by Vassil Panayotov, Guoguo Chen, Daniel Povey, and Sanjeev Khudanpur. The dataset is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
